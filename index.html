    How this check works
    Supported: caret ^1.2.3, tilde ~1.2, space-separated comparators >=1.2.0 <2.0.0, exact versions, partials, wildcards and || alternatives. Full versions follow SemVer precedence; build metadata does not affect ordering. Prereleases match only when each chosen clause explicitly mentions a prerelease with the same major.minor.patch tuple.
    Minimum possible and exact conflict pairs come from range intersections, independently of the available set. A coverage gap means the ranges overlap but no supplied version fits. OR alternatives may produce a higher-order conflict even when every pair overlaps. Hyphen ranges, commas, tags, npm aliases, v prefixes, parentheses and lockfile resolution are unsupported and rejected.
    Limits and grammar are documented in the local README. No installation, account, tracking, browser storage or network connection is required.
